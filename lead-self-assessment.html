Leadership Self-Assessment

This self-assessment is designed to help you reflect on your strengths and growth areas across Dartmouth's five leadership competency domains:

  • Self-Awareness & Personal Growth
  • Ethics & Integrity
  • Communication & Influence
  • Collaboration & Inclusion
  • Critical Thinking & Strategic Action

There are no right or wrong answers — this assessment is not graded or evaluated, and your results are for your own use and for anyone you choose to share them with. Your honest responses will generate a personalized leadership profile and recommendations for LE@D programs that align with your growth areas.

The assessment takes about 5 minutes. When you're finished, you'll be able to save and share your results.
