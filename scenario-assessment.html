LE@D Leadership Scenario Assessment

Explore how you lead across Dartmouth's five competency domains.

This assessment presents five realistic leadership scenarios; one for each LE@D domain. For each scenario, choose the response that best reflects how you would actually act, then use the reflection box to explain your thinking in a few sentences. Your written reflection is required and is just as important as your choice, as the personalized feedback you receive is generated from both.

There are no right or wrong answers. Your results are for your own use and reflection. The assessment takes about 10 minutes.
